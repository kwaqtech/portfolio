### Mobile Responsiveness Enhancement

The current codebase is stable. Now, focus on optimizing the UI/UX for mobile devices with the following improvements:

1. **Responsive Layout**

   * Ensure the entire website adapts smoothly to smaller screens (phones and tablets).
   * Adjust spacing, padding, and font sizes to improve readability and usability on mobile.
   * Avoid elements overflowing or being too tightly packed.

2. **Header Optimization for Mobile**

   * Keep the design clean and minimal.
   * Convert the navigation (**HOME, PROJECTS, ABOUT**) into a more mobile-friendly format (e.g., hamburger menu or compact layout if needed).
   * Keep the light/dark mode toggle and star animation toggle small and unobtrusive.
   * Ensure all controls are easy to tap (accessible touch targets).

3. **Project Sections & Components**

   * Make project cards stack vertically instead of horizontally.
   * Ensure images, icons, and text scale properly without distortion.
   * Maintain clear separation between sections for better visual flow.

4. **Performance & Interaction**

   * Optimize animations (including star animation) so they remain smooth on mobile devices.
   * Avoid heavy or unnecessary effects that may impact performance.

5. **Consistency Across Modes**

   * Ensure both light and dark modes remain visually clear and consistent on mobile.
   * Double-check contrast, readability, and spacing in both modes.

### Notes

* Prioritize usability and touch interaction.
* Keep the design clean, modern, and lightweight.
* Avoid major refactoring—focus on responsive adjustments only.
